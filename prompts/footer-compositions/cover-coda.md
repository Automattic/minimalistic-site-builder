**cover-coda** — ONE full-bleed wp:cover closes the page: a real
subject-matter background image under a strong dim (`"dimRatio"` of at
least 50 over a dark overlay color) with EXACTLY one short statement
heading over it — about the site's subject, never the site name restated.
Below the cover, ONE slim single-row utility baseline on the assigned
surface carries the identity, the links, and the credit on a single flex
row — never link columns, never a second content band. The cover background's AI_IMAGE
aspect-ratio is `landscape` or `ultrawide`, never `portrait`,
`card-portrait`, or `square` — a tall source is cropped to sludge in a wide
band.
