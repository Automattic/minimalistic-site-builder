**diptych** — EXACTLY two full-height slabs and one hard vertical seam: a
50/50 wp:columns row where each column carries its own solid palette
background and tall matched padding. One slab speaks identity — the site
title plus one short line; the other slab speaks utility — a compact link
stack, the credit, and at most one verified contact fact. The two fills must
differ decisively from each other and from the assigned root surface, so the
seam stays visible on every page. No image, no separator, no third column.
