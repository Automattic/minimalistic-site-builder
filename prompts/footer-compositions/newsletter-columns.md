**newsletter-columns** — The product-site footer: an INVITATION ROW over
THREE LINK COLUMNS, then one credit line. Build it as a wide `wp:columns` row
first: a 55/45 split whose leading column holds the site identity
(`wp:site-title`, one short line of two to twelve words about the site) and
whose trailing column holds ONE invitation — a level-3 `wp:heading` of two to
five words ("Stay in the loop", "Get the monthly note"), ONE line of at most
twelve words, and ONE `wp:buttons` with ONE `wp:button` whose destination the
FOOTER NAVIGATION/ACTION RULE allows (a real page or section, or the spec's
exact mailto); when no destination qualifies, keep the heading and the line
and omit the button. Below it, ONE `wp:columns` with THREE equal `wp:column`,
each a short caption-size label (a level-4 `wp:heading`, one or two words:
"Product", "Company", "Follow") over a `wp:navigation` of two to four links
that the same rule allows; when the site has fewer real destinations than
three columns need, use two columns and never invent a link. Close with ONE
credit paragraph at caption size. No fit-text line, no image, no form, no
social icons drawn as text.
