**photographic-split** — Build a deliberately unequal 60/40 or 65/35
composition using wp:media-text, a wp:cover-led image field, or two columns
with ONE generatable content image. Pair the image with one short brand
statement or genuinely useful action, then finish with a narrow full-width
utility baseline. Never shrink the image into a decorative thumbnail. The
image's AI_IMAGE aspect-ratio is `landscape`, `card-landscape`, or `square`,
never `portrait` or `card-portrait` — a tall image makes the band far taller
than its content and strands blank space beside the text column.
