**mosaic-tiles** — A hard-edged mosaic of flat color tiles: one identity
tile (the site title plus one short line), one solid tile in a strong
palette color carrying the compact link stack, and ONE content-image tile,
butted edge to edge with no gaps, no borders, and matched heights so the
band reads as a single printed plate. A thin full-width credit strip closes
the grid. Text on a colored tile uses the palette pairing that clears
contrast on that fill. The image tile's AI_IMAGE aspect-ratio is `square`
or `card-landscape`, never `portrait` or `card-portrait` — a tall tile
breaks the grid's matched heights.
