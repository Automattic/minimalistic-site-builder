**repeat-rail** — The identity as printed tape: ONE full-width rail repeats
the site's exact name three or four times on a single display-scale line,
separated by a plain middle dot, wrapping edge to edge. A second rail is
allowed only as a dimmed, offset echo of the first — a muted palette tone,
never a second full-contrast headline. Below the rails, ONE compact utility
baseline carries the links and the credit. No image, no fit-text device, no
third rail; the repetition count is fixed, never filler until the line is
full.
