**color-field** — One massive inset panel of a single strong palette color
(primary or accent) carries the ENTIRE composition: the identity at display
scale, one short statement, and a compact link stack, all in the one text
tone that clears contrast on that fill. The root surface stays visible as a
quiet mat around the panel, and the credit line sits on the mat below it.
No wp:button, no image, no second panel — the flat field of color is the
whole gesture.
