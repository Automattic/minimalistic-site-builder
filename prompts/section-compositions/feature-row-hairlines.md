### feature-row-hairlines

Build one row of three or four short text columns.
The theme supplies the borders between columns.

- Structure: Create one `wp:columns` row with `"align":"wide"`.
- Use three columns at 33.33% each, or four columns at 25% each.
- Copy budget: Start each column with one level-3 heading of two to four words.
- Put one or two short paragraphs after each heading, with at most two lines per paragraph.
- Keep these blocks directly inside their column.
- Put one section heading and at most one lead line before the row.
- Identity: Use the assigned root marker on the top-level group.

Use group, columns/column, heading, and paragraph blocks only.
- Media: Omit images, cards, buttons, separators, borders, and column padding.
The theme changes the column borders to horizontal borders on phones.

- Surface/width: The band runs wide.
- Objective failure: Multiple rows, long paragraphs, or card groups break this composition.
