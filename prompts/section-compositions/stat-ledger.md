### stat-ledger

Build one row of three or four figures with short labels.
Use only figures from the brief or SITE SPEC.

- Structure: Create one `wp:columns` row with `"align":"wide"`.
- Use three columns at 33.33% each, or four columns at 25% each.
- Copy budget: Start each column with one level-3 heading that contains only the figure and its sign or symbol.
- Put one paragraph with `"fontSize":"caption"` below each figure.
- Describe the figure in two to five words in that label.
- Put unit words in the label, separate from the figure.
- Use figures such as `120+`, `98%`, `$4.2M`, `1,200`, or `3x` only when the source supplies them.
- Put one section heading and at most one lead line before the row.
- Identity: Use the assigned root marker on the top-level group.

The theme sets the figure size and the borders between columns.
Use group, columns/column, heading, and paragraph blocks only.
- Media: Omit images, cards, separators, and figure font overrides.
Preserve available facts if the source supplies fewer than three figures; do not invent statistics to complete the row.

- Surface/width: The band runs wide.
- Objective failure: Multiple rows, fewer than three figures, or cards break this composition.
