### statement-lines

Build three to six short statements in a vertical sequence.
Each statement occupies one large line, with a border between lines.

- Structure: Create one `wp:group` with `"className":"statement-lines"` and `"layout":{"type":"constrained"}`.
- Put three to six level-3 headings inside that group.
- Copy budget: Use two to eight words for each heading.
- Write values, principles, disciplines, client names, or manifesto statements as the content requires.
- Put one section heading and at most one lead line before the group.
- Identity: Use the assigned root marker on the top-level group.

Use only headings inside the statement group.
- Surface/width: The band runs wide or at the content width.
The theme supplies line sizes, spaces, and borders.
- Media: Omit images, cards, columns, separators, and heading style overrides.
- Objective failure: Extra blocks inside the statement group or multiple statement groups break this composition.
