### centered-stack

Build one constrained column and let type scale and whitespace carry it. This
is the page's quiet beat, so it earns its band through restraint rather than
through a device. Do not rescue it with a rule, a box, or a decorative motif.

- Structure: one constrained column of headings and paragraphs. The theme
  centers the whole band through the root marker class, so author every
  element for a centered reading. Do not set a start alignment on a heading,
  a lead, or a paragraph. A long centered rag is hard to read, so keep each
  paragraph to two or three lines at the reading measure — trim the copy
  instead of realigning it.
- Copy budget: one heading, one lead line at most, and two or three short
  paragraphs. One `wp:buttons` when the section has a real next step.
- Identity: the one top-level group carries the assigned root marker class.
- Media: none, or one modest supporting image. Use group, heading, paragraph,
  list, quote, pullquote, image, and buttons.
- Surface/width: the whole band commits to content width. Do not widen one row
  and leave the rest at the reading measure — a half-wide stack reads as an
  accident.
- Objective failure: a heading or a paragraph forced back to start alignment
  inside the centered band (repeated item rows, lists, and a form the host
  substitutes in are the exceptions, and the theme handles them), a
  multi-column grid built inside the stack, or a
  hairline rule under the heading.
