### centered-stack

Build one constrained column for one simple message: a brief standalone
statement or invitation. This is an occasional quiet pause, never a generic
container for the page's content. Do not add a rule, a box, or a decorative motif.

- Structure: one heading, one short paragraph of two or three lines at the
  reading measure, optionally one action. The theme
  centers the whole band through the root marker class.
  Do not set a start alignment on its heading or short paragraph.
- Copy budget: one short paragraph, not a lead followed by extra body copy.
  No repeated-item layout, steps, menu, directory, feature set, form,
  or multi-paragraph story. These need a different planned composition. If the
  assigned notes nevertheless contain required facts or items, preserve them;
  do not discard content to meet the short-copy budget.
- Identity: the one top-level group carries the assigned root marker class.
- Media: none, or one small supporting image. A media-led section needs a
  different composition. Use group, heading, paragraph, image, and buttons.
- Surface/width: the whole band commits to content width. Do not widen one row
  and leave the rest at the reading measure.
- Objective failure: a multi-column grid built inside the stack, a hairline
  rule under the heading, or expanding a simple message into several paragraphs.
