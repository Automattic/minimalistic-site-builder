### panorama-rail

Pair one wide foreground visual field with a single compact information rail.
The rail is an intentionally narrow anchor, not a second equal column, and owns
the concise proposition plus any planned action. Preserve the panoramic crop
and strong horizontal rhythm on wide screens. On mobile the media remains an
ordinary image and the compact rail moves below it; never crop the panorama
into a tall cover or multiply the rail into dashboard-like panels.

- Structure: build one wide foreground `hero-composition__media` followed or
  offset by one compact `hero-composition__copy` rail inside the root group.
- Identity: the one root group carries exactly `.hero-composition--panorama-rail`.
- Media: exactly one wide landscape image, never a cover background. Use group,
  image, columns/column when needed for the rail, heading, paragraph, and an
  optional planned button only.
- Surface/width: use the planned solid base/tinted/contrast surface and the
  mixed-width-editorial projection; the media field is visibly wider than rail.
- Objective failure: a tall crop, equal media/copy widths, multiple rail panels,
  missing image, or mobile rail placed above the image fails this recipe.
