### focal-subject-stage

Stage one singular subject as an opaque foreground content image beside a
concise copy region, with scale and whitespace making the subject feel like an
exhibit. Request a normal image compatible with the JPG pipeline, never a
transparent cutout. The copy may carry one planned action but cannot compete
with the focal object. Mobile preserves the subject's prominence and stacks in
the blueprint's order; never turn the subject into wallpaper or a tiny card.

- Structure: one unequal columns/media-text stage pairs one concise
  `hero-composition__copy` region with one dominant `hero-composition__media`.
- Identity: the one root group carries exactly `.hero-composition--focal-subject-stage`.
- Media: exactly one opaque foreground JPG-compatible image with a singular
  subject; never request transparency or a cutout. Use group, columns/column or
  media-text, image, heading, paragraph, and optional planned button only.
- Surface/width: keep the planned solid base/tinted/contrast surface and the
  asymmetric-split projection; image scale must remain the dominant region.
- Objective failure: a cover/background image, transparent asset, multiple
  subjects/frames, tiny card treatment, or copy obscuring the subject fails.
