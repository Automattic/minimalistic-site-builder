### statement-type

Let the headline itself be the hero: one statement set in display-scale type
across the full wide measure, on a bare solid surface with no media anywhere in
the section. The composition's drama comes from the scale jump between the
statement and its small supporting cluster, and from generous, deliberate
negative space — not from ornament, texture, or imagery. This is the one recipe
whose first screen cannot be weakened by a bad photograph; the words must
therefore be strong enough to carry it alone.

- Structure: one root group with a single bounded `hero-composition__copy`
  region holding the whole composition; no `hero-composition__media` region
  exists in this recipe.
- Statement discipline: the level-1 heading spans the wide measure and breaks
  into two to four intentional lines. Size it so its longest word holds on one
  line at the largest heading preset that fits (uppercase and wide-tracked
  type break soonest); stepping down one preset beats letting a word wrap.
  The statement says something only this brand could say — it never restates
  the site title the header already shows directly above it.
- Copy budget: exactly one level-1 heading, at most ONE short supporting
  paragraph, and at most one planned button. The paragraph and button form one
  small subordinate cluster clearly separated from the statement by scale and
  spacing — no eyebrow above the heading, no hairline rules, no caption or
  credit lines.
- Vertical stage: the composition breathes — the root carries the `lg` spacing
  preset top AND bottom (the copy-led solid-surface case), and the statement
  never crowds the header. Do not simulate height with empty spacer stacks.
- Identity: the one root group carries exactly `.hero-composition--statement-type`.
- Media: none. No image, cover, video, or gallery block anywhere in the
  section. Use only group, heading, paragraph, buttons/button, and spacer when
  structurally needed.
- Surface/width: keep the planned solid contrast/tinted/base surface on the
  root, running `"align":"full"` edge-to-edge; type and button colors come
  from the palette with headline contrast at the readable maximum.
- Objective failure: any image or cover block, an eyebrow or kicker line, a
  decorative rule, more than one supporting paragraph, or a headline confined
  to a narrow centered card instead of the wide measure fails.
