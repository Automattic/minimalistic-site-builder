### triptych

Open on a body of work: a concise copy block, then three images in one row at
ONE shared crop. Three frames at the same ratio read as a series a visitor can
compare; three frames at three ratios read as a collage, which this recipe
refuses. The images are the subject and the copy introduces them, so the copy
stays short enough to sit above the row without competing with it. Mobile
stacks the copy first, then the three frames in order, each still at the shared
crop.

- Structure: the `hero-composition__copy` region first, then ONE row holding
  three equal `hero-composition__media` frames — a three-column `wp:columns`
  with `"align":"wide"`, or one `wp:gallery` with `"columns":3`.
- Copy budget: one level-1 heading, at most ONE supporting paragraph, and at
  most one planned button. No caption under the frames and no per-frame label.
- Identity: the one root group carries exactly `.hero-composition--triptych`.
- Media: exactly THREE foreground images. Every image request ends with the
  same aspect word, and that word is the same for all three — pick one crop
  for the set and hold it. Never a `wp:cover`, never a background image, never
  a transparent cutout, and never a fourth frame to balance the row.
- One subject per frame, three related subjects across the set: they must read
  as three works from one practice, not three views of one object and not
  three unrelated stock scenes.
- Surface: keep the planned solid base/tinted/contrast surface. The frames need
  the quiet field around them; do not paint the root with an image.
- Objective failure: more or fewer than three images, mixed aspects across the
  set, a cover background, per-frame captions, or the row collapsed into a
  single feature image with two thumbnails fails this recipe.
