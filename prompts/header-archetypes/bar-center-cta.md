Top-level wp:group (`"layout":{"type":"constrained"}`, a `backgroundColor` per the SEAM rule, the same `sm` padding) holding ONE inner wp:group with `"className":"header-bar-center"`, `"align":"wide"` and `"layout":{"type":"flex","flexWrap":"nowrap","justifyContent":"space-between","verticalAlignment":"center"}`. Inside, in this order: wp:site-title (or wp:site-logo), ONE wp:navigation with at most 5 links, then ONE wp:buttons holding ONE wp:button — the site's primary action, label 1-2 words. The trusted kit centers the navigation and rounds the CTA: never author widths, a second row, or a tagline. Suits sober product landings and portfolio sites that want a classic bar with a clear action.
