### card

Render the repeated items as discrete cards. This is the familiar catalog
idiom: every item has its own bounded reading unit, with consistent anatomy and
equal internal rhythm. Do not reduce the set to bare heading-plus-paragraph
columns.

- Put `item-pattern__item` on every repeated item's outer `wp:group`.
- When an item pairs an image with its copy, follow the ASSIGNED CARD STYLE
  anatomy and place its matching `card-style--*` marker beside
  `item-pattern__item`. Text-only cards may use a simple surface, padding, and
  border without pretending to be image cards.
- Use the assigned section archetype for the overall topology. An
  `equal-card-grid` stays equal; a mixed or split composition may make one card
  dominant, but every item still reads as a self-contained card.
- Keep one heading and one compact detail block per item. Do not add eyebrow
  labels merely to make every card have three lines.
