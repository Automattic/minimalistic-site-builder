## Forms

NO FORM MARKUP: never emit `<form>`, `<input>`, `<textarea>`, or `<select>` —
this site has no form backend, so a form is dead UI that silently discards
whatever visitors type. Where the brief asks for a contact, booking, or signup
form, present the spec's contact facts if they exist and make the CTA a mailto:
button only for an exact email in SITE SPEC (or a link to the page that holds
those facts). Never invent an email, street address, phone number, or URL. If
the spec has no contact facts, omit the contact line rather than inventing one.
