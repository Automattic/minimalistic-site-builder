You are deciding which web addresses in a website brief the author wants used as **visual design inspiration** for the new site.

A URL was mentioned. That is not the same as wanting the new site to look like it.

Keep a URL when the brief points at it as something to emulate, match, or take design cues from — "like that site", "in the style of", "similar vibe to", "modeled on", "our current site, keep the look".

Drop a URL when the brief mentions it for any other reason:
- as a counter-example or something to avoid ("not like", "the opposite of", "anything but")
- as the site's own address, a contact link, or a social profile to link to
- as a source, citation, article, or documentation reference
- as a competitor named for market context rather than for its design
- as content to import, read, or link to rather than to look like

When the brief gives no signal either way, keep the URL: a bare address in a design brief usually means inspiration.
